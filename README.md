# Assignment 4: Clustering Analysis & Industry Segmentation

## Industry/Domain Relevance
My career is in the power generation and distribution industry. 

## Dataset Description and Source
I used Kaggle's Combined Cycle Power Plant (CCPP) dataset for the previous assignments. In order to perform clustering analyses, I chose a dataset with some discrete variables, rather than all continuous variables like the CCPP dataset.

The UCI HAR IMU dataset is used to complement the Combined Cycle Power Plant (CCPP) analyses performed during the previous assignments. The application of advanced control algorithms to devices, such as variable frequency drives (VFDs), valves, and regulators, make this dataset relevant to the power generation and distribution industry. The features used for the supervised task are acceleration in the x,y,z directions, obtained from an IMU on a smartphone, and the labels are different activities performed while wearing said smartphone.

Valves might have automated components, and maybe the analysis of maintenance profiles could benefit from the ability to cluster valves based on several different parameters: location, volumetric flow rate, temperature of the water, etc.

## Summary
I've worked on analyses of datasets generated by IMUs before and I was surprised at the initial exploratory analysis of the UCI HAR IMU dataset. The activities lent themselves to semi-discrete clusters in 3 dimensions. The most obvious cluster was between the smartphone being either in the [VERTICAL] or [HORIZONTAL] orientation. For the purposes of the assignment, I discontinued the analysis at 2 clusters. With further exploration, it could be possible to cluster the activities in the [VERTICAL] position.
